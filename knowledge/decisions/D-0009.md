# D-0009 — Meat by its size, and the single tag

**The ask.** Rusty, 2026-10-26, on Heft's tags (its D-0006) piling every steak: "some meats should
stack (like little chops or slices of bacon or whatever) but larger stuff should be singular. Like
a whole steak, or a baked chicken, etc".

**Why a new tag.** Tags only add. Every meat and fish was in `stackable_cluster` by name and again
through `#c:foods/raw_meat`, `cooked_meat`, `raw_fish` and `cooked_fish`, so taking a name out of
the cluster tag changed nothing, and another mod (Heft) could only move an item from cluster to
pile, never to one to a spot.

**Decision.**

- `#setitdown:stackable_single`: one to a spot whatever the pile and cluster tags say. `kind` reads
  armour, then single, then pile, then cluster.
- **Small cuts share a spot**: pork chops and Farmer's Delight's bacon, mutton chops, patty, mince
  and fish slices pile (flat); its chicken cuts heap.
- **A whole joint is one to a spot**: beef, mutton (vanilla's is a leg), chicken, rabbit, raw and
  cooked; whole fish (cod, salmon, tropical fish, pufferfish); Farmer's Delight's ham and smoked ham.
  Whole fish are our reading of "larger stuff", not Rusty's words.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *Take the `c:` food tags out of the cluster tag.* Every modded meat would then be one to a spot,
  the small cuts with it, and every modded vegetable that came through the same kind of tag.
- *Lower the cluster's precedence below "none".* There is no "none" without a tag to name it.
