# D-0002 — Support, room, the clamp, and the give

**Decision**, the README's three server fixes taken up and two of its gaps closed:

- **What it may rest on.** A support needs a collision shape, or a layer of snow (one layer has no
  collision). That refuses water, lava, flowers, torches and cobwebs, and keeps leaves, fences,
  slabs and carpets. The README's rule was "not air", which let a display ride on lava.
- **While it stays.** Every 10 ticks (staggered by id) the display checks that its support's shape
  still has a face in the display's plane under its point. When the block is broken, exploded,
  flooded, pushed by a piston (the head moves into the cell, and "not air" would have left the
  display floating over the arm) or swapped for something shorter, it drops what it holds.
  `doEntityDrops` is respected. An explosion near it drops it, a piston pushing it drops it, and
  projectiles pass through.
- **Where on the face.** The region a display may lie on is built from the block's outline shape:
  the boxes whose face lies in the clicked plane, less what stands out past it. A stair's lower
  step takes it only on the half its upper step leaves open. The centre is clamped so the display's
  half-block square stays on that region, by its reach at the current turn: 0.25 square, 0.354 at
  45 degrees. That is the README's corner overhang, fixed. It is re-clamped when turned; the move
  rides on the turn's own sync.
- **Room.** A different item within a quarter block of a display is refused ("No room here"), and
  so is the same item that does not pile. The same item that piles joins, up to `maxStackedItems`.
  The README allowed any overlap, which z-fights. Each display is also pushed a hair further out by
  its id, so even displays a little apart never fight over a pixel.
- **The give.** An item taken back goes through `Carried.giveOrDrop`: into the inventory or a
  carried bag (bags first where they already hold some), with only what fits nowhere dropped at the
  player's feet. This is the house rule for every give. The README dropped it on the floor with a
  five-minute life, then proposed `placeItemBackInInventory`, which misses bags.
- **The config is the server's.** `setitdown-server.toml` is a SERVER config, synced to every
  client, so nobody's prediction disagrees with the server. The README's COMMON config had to be
  shipped to both sides by hand. `requireTwoHits` defaults to true, as the README recommends for a
  shared world.

**Verified.** GameTests cover water, lava, a flower and a torch refused; the support broken,
flooded, exploded and pistoned; the stair; the edge and its re-clamp; no room; a pile to four and
full; the give into the inventory and onto a worn Backpacks+ bag's own ingots; and creative.
Mutation runs (`devtools/verification/mutations-1.0.0.py`), each caught by the tests named:

- A: no room refusal. Caught by `PlacementTest` twice and `anythingElseWithinAQuarterBlockHasNoRoom`.
- B: "not air". Caught by the flooded, piston and nothing-to-rest-on tests.
- C: a floor drop. Caught by the two-hit, worn-bag and one-hit tests.
- D: the clamp without the turn. Caught by `FacePlacementTest` and the edge GameTest.
