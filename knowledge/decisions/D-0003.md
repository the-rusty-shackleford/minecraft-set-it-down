# D-0003 — Armour on an invisible stand

**Decision**, the README's design kept:

- **The stand.** A piece of armour (`ArmorItem` in a head, chest, legs or feet slot, or an elytra;
  never horse or wolf armour) is drawn worn by an invisible armour stand with no base plate. The
  stand is one per display, never added to the world and never ticked, and is drawn through the
  game's `EntityRenderDispatcher`. Whatever the player's packs do to armour on a stand applies:
  EMF with Armored Legacy's `armor_stand_*.jem`, trims, dyes, glint and modded armour. Invisible,
  it draws only what it wears and casts no shadow.
- **Where it touches.** That comes from one measuring render per item into a box capture, the same
  capture the items use, adapted from Quick Slot's. The pose is the domain's `Pose.armour`:
  - On a floor it stands, facing whoever set it down, turning about the vertical. Its tip pitches
    it forward onto its face and lowers it until it rests.
  - Under a ceiling it hangs upright, its top touching.
  - On a wall it hangs facing out, its back to the wall. Its turn rolls it about the wall's normal,
    and a tip is ignored.
- **Never piles.** Its box is roughly the piece's size (0.6 across, its height up the floor or
  along the wall).

**Verified.** `PoseTest` covers resting, pitching, hanging and facing out on every wall. The
GameTests cover the floor, the wall, the wall's ignored tip, the floor's pitch and no pile. The
booth photographs every piece standing, hung, under a ceiling and pitched, under EMF with Armored
Legacy and Complementary, and checks the measured sizes: a chestplate a body's height, a helmet a
head's.
