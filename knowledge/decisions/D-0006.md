# D-0006 — At its own size

**The ask.** The same friend's feedback (2026-10-07): "Pistol & shield are unrealistically small,
should do a pass on other items that might be under sized."

**Why.** D-0004 sizes an item by the side of the square it fills. 1.0.0 multiplied that side into
the item-frame view's model, which some models shrink to fit a frame:

| Model | Item-frame scale | What 1.0.0 drew |
|---|---|---|
| A block | 0.5 | a quarter-block cube, not the half its size says |
| A shield (also a chest) | 0.5 | a shield 0.48 tall |
| The Ranged Weapons Mod's pistol | 0.45 | 0.12 long, smaller than a compass |

Sprites, which fill the frame, were as designed.

**Decision.**

- **The item's own model** is drawn at its own size, sixteen of its pixels to the square: its
  item-frame scale and translation are dropped (D-0005), its turn is kept. It is shrunk only if it
  would not fit the square (a shield 22 pixels tall), and centred on it. A block is now the
  half-block cube D-0004 says.
- **A model only the hand draws** (D-0005) is drawn as big as the item's own model shows in an
  item frame, measured by a sprite's opaque pixels (its edges ring them):
  - its longest way is matched to that picture's longest side;
  - laid corner to corner, it is matched to the picture's diagonal, which is how long a sprite's
    sword runs.

  Then it is shrunk into its square like any other.
- **A whole block's box is its cube.** A block item whose block is a whole cube
  (`Hitbox.Piece.BLOCK`) has a box as deep as it is wide (its diagonal leaning), so the crosshair
  finds the cube it is drawn as.
- **The tags.** The shield (and `#c:tools/shield`) moves from tool to **long**: as big as a sword
  is long, at 0.8. The pistol and the revolver move from small to **normal** (0.5): a book's size,
  not a compass's. Magazines stay small.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *A hand model at the pack's own size, sixteen pixels to the square.* Fresh Food's apple is
  built 7.4 pixels tall: 0.12 of a block, a crumb beside the sprite it replaced (the booth's
  photo).
- *Matching by the extent across the face.* A standing apple's is its base: it grew to 1.26 of its
  square. Found by the booth's measurement, now a check.
- *Matching a long model to the picture's side.* A slanted sword fell a quarter short of the sword
  it replaced.
- *Filling the square with every model's whole extent.* A resource pack's small 3D nugget would
  grow to its whole square, bigger than its sprite's pixels ever were.

**Verification.**

- JUnit:
  - a 27-pixel sword is shrunk into its square and centred;
  - a hand's 3D apple matches its sprite standing or lying;
  - a slanted sword matches the sprite's diagonal;
  - a whole block's box is its cube, leaning its diagonal;
  - each rule's mutation is caught.
- GameTests: the shield is long; a log is a whole block with a box half a block deep, not armour;
  a chest and a torch are not.
- The booth checks a log's whole cube, a shield whole, the pistol at its own size, and the hand's
  apple as tall as its sprite. It photographs the size line-up with the shield and the pistol
  added.
