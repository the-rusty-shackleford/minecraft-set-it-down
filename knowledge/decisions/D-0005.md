# D-0005 — The hand's model

**The ask.** A friend's feedback on 1.0.0, passed on by Rusty (2026-10-07): "Some models are
defaulting to 2d sprite model when 3d model exists from resource packs. Ex, all tools / weapons,
some food like the apple. Would be best if set down items were consistent with their 3d model
equipped counterparts. With resource packs taking effect."

**Why it happened.**

- The pack carries Modefite, which reads newer versions' item definitions (`assets/<ns>/items/`)
  on 1.21.1. Fresh Food and Refined Tools give their 3D models through those definitions, by the
  view an item is drawn in: the sprite in `gui`, `ground` and `fixed`, the 3D model in the hands.
- 1.0.0 drew a display in the `fixed` (item-frame) view, so it got the sprite.
- 1.0.0's booth never showed it, for two reasons:
  - it fetched Fresh Food and Refined Tools but not Modefite;
  - its options listed no incompatible packs, so the game dropped Fresh Food (a pack made for
    newer versions) at start. Rusty's game lists it in `incompatibleResourcePacks`.

**Decision.**

- **The hand's view.** A display's item is drawn through the item renderer in the third-person
  hand's view, so the model a hand shows is what is drawn: a resource pack's through Modefite,
  NeoForge's per-view models, the trident's and the spyglass's own.
- **A resting pose.** The hand's pose is dropped and a resting one put in its place.
  - A client mixin wraps the item renderer's call to `ClientHooks.handleCameraTransforms`.
  - It acts only on a render `HeldView` asked for, once; every other item render passes
    through.
- **The item's own model** (what an item frame would draw) is turned as an item frame turns it.
  The item frame's translation and scale are dropped (D-0006).
- **A model only the hand draws** is built to be held. Each is built upright in its own frame, as
  it would sit on a plate, so (`domain/Rest`):
  - It **stands on its own base where it could stand**: no taller than twice its narrowest width.
    Fresh Food's apple stands, stem up; its loaf, cookie and steak lie flat on their bases.
  - **Anything taller would fall over**, so it lies on its broadest face, its thinnest dimension
    out of the surface: a carrot on its side, a sword on its flat, a melon slice on its flat
    side. A tie of side and front goes to its front.
  - **A long one is laid corner to corner**, its far end up and to the right, as the item's sprite
    drew it: a sword, a carrot. Long means half again as long as wide across the face, where the
    item's own picture is not long.
- **"Only the hand draws"** means the model the hand view picks is not the one the item-frame view
  would. Measurements are cached per own model and per the stack's components, since an item
  definition may choose by a custom name or an enchantment. A slanted model is measured again as
  drawn: its box turned 45 degrees would hold far more than it does.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *The 3D model's own item-frame transform.* It is dead data in these packs, which never show the
  3D model in a frame. Refined Tools' would stand its sword edge-on.
- *One fixed turn for every hand model* (the sprite's). A cookie would stand on edge, a carrot on
  end.
- *Always the broadest face.* The booth's apple lay on its side and read as a red block.
- *The head view* (the 3D model too, in both packs). Its transforms are arbitrary: Refined Tools
  tilts its sword 25 degrees.
- *Calling Modefite.* It would cover one mod; the hand view covers every way a model is chosen by
  view.

**Verification.**

- JUnit:
  - each pack model's rest: an apple stands, a carrot and a melon slice lie, a sword lies on its
    flat, and the tie order;
  - 300 random boxes, where what cannot stand lies thinnest-out;
  - which models are slanted, and the slant's direction.
- The booth:
  - It now fetches Modefite, the Ranged Weapons Mod and Metals and Materials, and lists Fresh Food
    as Rusty's game does.
  - It checks that the hand's 3D apple (standing) and sword (slanted) are what is drawn, and that
    paper is drawn by its own model.
  - It photographs a bench of 3D models beside the same apple and sword held in the hand.
