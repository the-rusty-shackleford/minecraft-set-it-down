# D-0004 — Relative sizes

**The ask.** Rusty, watching the first booth on 2026-10-07: "Mounted swords seem pretty small, like
they are the same size as a compass. Maybe full size is too large, but can we do a better job with
relative sizing?" An item frame draws every item at the same half block, because each item's
sprite fills the same 16-pixel square. The README did the same.

**Decision.** Each item has a size, the side of the square its model fills, in blocks. It comes
from the first of four item tags it is in; anything in none is normal.

| Size | Side | Tag | Seeded with |
|---|---|---|---|
| long | 0.8 | `#setitdown:size/long` | swords, tridents, bows, crossbows, maces, RWM's long guns and launcher |
| tool | 0.7 | `#setitdown:size/tool` | pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, fishing rods, shields, wrenches |
| normal | 0.5 | (none) | everything else: books, food, blocks, buckets, maps |
| small | 0.375 | `#setitdown:size/small` | compasses, clocks, ingots, gems, raw ore, apples, potatoes, eggs, pearls, RWM's pistols |
| tiny | 0.3 | `#setitdown:size/tiny` | nuggets, seeds, dusts, berries, rounds |

- **Long weapons.** A sword's blade runs the sprite's diagonal, so at 0.8 it is about 0.85 of a
  block long. That is short of the full size Rusty thought too large.
- **Small and tiny things** are smaller than an item frame's half but still readable. They are
  never true to scale: a true compass would be a dot beside a sword.
- **What else follows the size.** Its box (the square on the face; tipped, nine tenths of its side
  deep, never under the README's 0.45), its edge clamp (the reach is half its side, or half its
  diagonal turned 45 degrees) and its pile's spread.
- **The player's own scale.** `itemScale` scales all of it, its 0.5 being as designed.
- **Armour** keeps the worn piece's own size.

**Verified.**
- JUnit covers a long item's reach and its clamp (turned 45 degrees it is wider than a face, so it
  takes the face's middle), a tiny one's, the boxes, and a cluster's spread in proportion.
- The GameTest `aSwordIsSetDownBiggerThanACompass` reads each size off the tags and measures the
  boxes: a sword 0.8, a compass 0.375.
- The booth films the line-up (sword, pickaxe, book, compass, nugget) and the wall Rusty saw.
